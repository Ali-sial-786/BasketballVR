# Sources and asset terms

- **Crease VR / Cricket**, the user's repository, default branch `ccr-544f4cdd-fs8lqk`: `src/physics/math.js`, the reference-space controller motion approach, session lifecycle, humanoid skinning/IK, animation player, and offline athlete conversion are reused/adapted. Cricket declares MIT code licensing. Cricket-specific gameplay, commentary and cricket gear are not shipped.
- **Courtside**, the user's repository: the original athlete pipeline and support-hand conversion are adapted in `tools/rebuild_anatomy.py` and `tools/add_support_grip.py`. The basketball derivative retains complete skin under the kit, anatomical eyes/jaw, adult proportions, a draped sleeveless jersey and loose shorts, shoes, hair, modeled facial details, baked ambient occlusion and two runtime LODs.
- **MakeHuman HM08 anatomy, skeleton, skin weights and macro targets**: CC0-1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. Source revision `a8bc2d54ff0ac92e78ff71431b1023eda42bf482` in https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman. `tools/fetch_anatomy.py` pins and verifies the source inputs. Runtime model: `public/assets/models/athlete.json` and `athlete.bin`.
- **MakeHuman 1.1 system assets: skin photos, eyes, eyebrows and eyelashes**: CC0-1.0 (MakeHuman's licence releases its base assets under CC0; the textures carry a PUBLICDOMAIN stamp; some text files keep a legacy AGPL header). Fetched and checksum-verified by `tools/fetch_anatomy.py`, processed by `tools/build_skin_textures.py` and `tools/rebuild_anatomy.py`:
  - Skin: `young_lightskinned_male_diffuse.png` and `young_darkskinned_male_diffuse.png` (`skins/textures/`), https://github.com/dmaugis/makehuman-py3-assets (revision `2f4033a364a7e97479e17ed630ccb999395730c6`, a mirror of the MakeHuman system assets; textures through Git LFS). Shipped as `public/assets/textures/skin_light_body.jpg`, `skin_light_face.jpg`, `skin_dark_body.jpg`, `skin_dark_face.jpg` (body layout resized to 1024, the head's UV island cropped at full density). The short afro photographed onto the dark-skinned texture's scalp is lifted into `hair.png` (strand detail and coverage, its front hairline extended) and that scalp replaced with recoloured shaved scalp from the light-skinned texture.
  - Eyes: the high-poly eye proxy `eyes/high-poly/high-poly.obj` and `.mhclo` from https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman (revision `a8bc2d54ff0ac92e78ff71431b1023eda42bf482`, `makehuman/data/`), without its cornea shell; texture `eyes/materials/brownlight_eye.png` from the mirror above.
  - Eyebrows: `eyebrows/eyebrow001/eyebrow001.obj`, `.mhclo` and `eyebrow001.png`; eyelashes: `eyelashes/eyelashes01/eyelashes01.obj`, `.mhclo` and `eyelashes01.png` (lower lashes at half coverage), from the mirror above.
  - The eye, eyebrow and eyelash textures are packed into `public/assets/textures/face_detail.png`; the fitted geometry is baked into `athlete.json` / `athlete.bin`.
- **Quaternius Universal Animation Library**, CC0-1.0, https://quaternius.com: idle, walk, jog, sprint, defense, throw, cheer and sad clips originally retargeted by Crease VR from Courtside’s `sportsmate.glb`. Included in `public/assets/anim/clips.*`.
- **Mixamo / Adobe, user-supplied `Dribble _2_.fbx`**: a 2.1-second dribble take provided in this conversation. Only the retargeted motion embedded in this game is included in `clips.bin`; the raw FBX, its character and its textures are not distributed. This clip retains Mixamo/Adobe’s applicable asset terms, not MIT/CC0. Do not extract or redistribute it as a standalone animation asset. The converter is `tools/import_dribble.mjs`.
- **Barlow Condensed** by Jeremy Tribby and **DM Sans** by Colophon Foundry and contributors: SIL Open Font License. Self-hosted under `public/fonts/`; the license texts are included there.
- **Spectators** (`public/assets/models/crowd.json`, `crowd.bin`): derived by `tools/build_crowd.mjs` from this game's own athlete model (MakeHuman HM08, CC0-1.0): posed, re-dressed by body region and simplified. No additional source.
- **Hall HDRI** (`public/assets/textures/hall.hdr`): the “hall” environment from https://github.com/pmndrs/assets (`src/hdri/hall.hdr`), a selection of Poly Haven HDRIs; repository licence CC0-1.0. Downsampled to 512×256 for this game.
- **Wood floor detail** (`wood_color.jpg`, `wood_normal.jpg`, `wood_rough.jpg`): ambientCG **WoodFloor040**, CC0-1.0, obtained from the mirror https://github.com/Papyszoo/CC0-Public-Domain-Textures (512 px KTX2) and transcoded to JPEG. Used as plank relief, gloss variation and grain under the game's own painted court.
- **Uniform lettering**: an original block alphabet and numerals defined as stroke paths in `src/render/kitprint.js`; no font file is used or distributed. Names and numbers are fictional.
- **Knit detail** (`jersey_normal.jpg`): Poly Haven **cotton_jersey** normal map, CC0-1.0, from the same mirror, resized to 256 px.
- Considered and not shipped: the CC0 basketball in GodotVR/godot-xr-tools (its textures carry Godot branding), Kenney's CC0 furniture (stylised), MakeHuman's CC0 clothes and its card-based hair proxies (`short01`–`04`, `afro01`: long or full styles; the photographed short hair reads better for athletes), and the CMU motion-capture basketball trials (free to use; they need a BVH retarget). See `REFERENCE_NOTES.md`.
- **Three.js**: MIT. **Vite**, **Playwright** and **meshoptimizer** retain their own package licenses. Dependency versions are pinned in `package-lock.json`.
- Arena layout, hardwood art, lighting, seating, scene props, UI, ball graphics and synthesized audio are original to this game. No NBA branding, game assets, player likenesses, recorded voices or commercial game audio are used. The jump shot's mechanics follow published descriptions of Klay Thompson's shooting form; his name and likeness appear nowhere in the game. NBA 2K, Wii Sports basketball and NBA Playgrounds are gameplay inspirations only. Reference links and the footage review are in `REFERENCE_NOTES.md`; reference media is not redistributed.
